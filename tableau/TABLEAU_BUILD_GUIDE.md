# Tableau Build Guide — Project 7 Final Approved Dashboard

## Design
- White background
- Blue + orange analytical colors
- Thin light-gray borders
- MM/YY date format
- Six KPI cards
- Exactly six analytical visualizations

## KPI Cards
1. Total Customers
2. Financial Crime Alerts
3. Escalated Cases
4. SAR Filed
5. Repeat-Alert Customers
6. High-Risk Exposure

## Six Approved Visualizations
1. Financial Crime Activity Trend — multi-series trend lines
2. Alerts by Risk Category — donut chart
3. Alerts by Region — geographic map with Top 5 Regions
4. Customer Risk Activity Heatmap — customer segment by month, values inside cells
5. Investigation Funnel — proper descending funnel from Total Alerts → Reviewed → Escalated → Investigated → SAR Filed
6. Crime Exposure by Product Type — stacked bar chart

## Color Standard
Use blue and orange only for analytical marks, with navy/dark blue headings and text.
