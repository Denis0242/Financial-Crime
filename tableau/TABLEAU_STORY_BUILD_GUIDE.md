# Tableau Story — Financial Crime Intelligence & Risk Analytics

## Story title
**Financial Crime Intelligence & Risk Analytics: From Signals to Investigation Priorities**

## Story sequence
1. **Executive Overview** — 6 KPIs + 6 approved visuals.
2. **Financial Crime Risk Trends** — alert, high-risk, and escalation movement over time.
3. **Customer Risk Segmentation** — risk-level distribution, repeat-alert customers, and multi-risk customers.
4. **Geographic / Regional Risk** — countries with concentrated alerted dollars and escalations.
5. **Product & Channel Exposure** — risk exposure by ACH, wire, P2P, cash, card, and check products.
6. **Investigation Funnel** — true descending alert → reviewed → escalated → investigated → SAR filed funnel.
7. **Repeat-Alert & Counterparty Intelligence** — repeat-alert customers and common counterparties.
8. **Key Findings & Recommended Actions** — analyst conclusions and next-step recommendations.

## Tableau build approach
Create one dashboard per story point, then create a Tableau Story and add each dashboard in the order above.

### Design standard
- White background
- Blue + orange only for analytical marks
- Navy headings/text
- Thin light-gray borders
- MM/YY date format
- Keep the analysis at Financial Crime Analyst / analytics level

## Story files
The `/images/story/` folder contains a visual reference for every story point.
